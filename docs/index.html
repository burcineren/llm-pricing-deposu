Adds a Monthly column and sorts by it. Long-context surcharges are applied when the request crosses a provider's threshold. Cached tokens bill at cache_read where the provider publishes one, and at the normal input rate otherwise — cache writes are not modelled, so a cache-heavy workload will run slightly higher than shown. Batch and priority tiers are ignored.

Aggregated from public sources on a best-effort basis and sometimes wrong. Enterprise agreements, committed-spend discounts and free tiers are out of scope. Confirm on the provider's own pricing page — every row links to it — before making a purchasing decision.
